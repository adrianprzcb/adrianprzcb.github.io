Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Fate Android application (the “App”) handles information when you install or use it.

The App is provided by RUSHERS ADR (the “Service Provider,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, RUSHERS ADR is the controller of personal data processed for its own purposes.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account and does not ask you to submit your name, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your selected language, daily fate, notification settings, and cached ad-free status are stored locally on your device.
  • The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general app usage and performance.
  • The App uses Google AdMob to load and display interstitial advertisements.
  • An optional one-time purchase to remove ads is processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank details.
  • Notifications are optional and scheduled locally on your device.
  • When you share a fate, the App creates a temporary image on your device and sends it only to the app or service you select through Android’s share interface.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to the Daily Fate Android App and related support communications with the Service Provider. It does not govern third-party apps, websites, advertising services, payment services, or sharing destinations. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information in Android app-private storage so its features work correctly:

  • Language settings: whether a language has been selected and the currently selected language.
  • Daily fate information: the date, category, title, message, and advice for the fate revealed on your device.
  • Notification preferences: whether reminders are enabled, whether notification permission has been requested, the selected reminder time, and the language used for notifications.
  • Purchase entitlement cache: a local true-or-false value indicating whether Google Play has reported that the ad-free upgrade is owned.
  • Temporary sharing file: a PNG image of your fate generated in the App’s cache when you use the sharing feature.

This locally stored information is used to provide the App’s features. The Service Provider does not operate a server that receives your selected fate, language, or notification schedule.

Local information normally remains on your device until it is replaced, cleared through the App or Android settings, removed automatically from the cache, or deleted when the App is uninstalled. Android backup behavior may also depend on your device and Google account backup settings.

4. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

The App includes third-party software development kits supplied by Google. These services may automatically collect or receive information when the App is installed, opened, or used.

4.1 Google Analytics for Firebase

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically process information such as:

  • a pseudonymous app-instance identifier;
  • the Android Advertising ID, when available;
  • masked IP address and approximate geographic information derived from it;
  • app opens, sessions, screen views, and other app lifecycle events;
  • device type, operating system, app version, language, and general technical information;
  • in-app purchase events, including product information and price; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

The App does not create a Daily Fate account or intentionally assign your name or email address as a Firebase user ID. Analytics information is used to understand general usage, measure performance, diagnose issues, and improve the App.

4.2 Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK

The App uses Google AdMob to request and display interstitial advertisements. According to Google’s disclosures, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share:

  • your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and advertisement interactions, such as app launches, taps, impressions, and video views;
  • diagnostic information, including app or SDK performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other identifiers when available.

Google may use this information for advertising, ad delivery, frequency control, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google’s configuration, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or based on contextual information.

Buying the optional ad-free upgrade prevents advertisements from being shown once the entitlement is recognized. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still initialize as part of the App.

4.3 Google Play Services and Google Play Billing

The App connects to Google Play to obtain product details, display the locally applicable price, process the optional ad-free purchase, acknowledge the purchase, and check whether the purchase is owned or pending.

Google processes your payment method, billing information, Google account information, and transaction under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. The App may receive product information, purchase status, and a purchase token or transaction identifier required to verify and acknowledge the purchase. The Service Provider does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. The App stores only a local cached value indicating whether the ad-free feature is owned.

5. Information You Choose to Provide

The App does not include account registration, a profile, or a form for submitting personal information to the Service Provider.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to answer your request, provide support, protect the App, or comply with legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

6. Notifications

The App may ask for Android notification permission. If you allow notifications, the App schedules a daily reminder locally at the time you select. Notification preferences and the selected language are stored on your device. The App also uses Android’s boot-completed functionality to restore enabled reminders after a device restart.

Notification permission is optional. You can turn reminders off in the App’s settings or change notification permission in Android settings. If today’s fate has already been revealed, the App is designed not to send that day’s reminder.

7. Sharing Your Fate

When you tap the share button, the App creates a temporary PNG card in its private cache containing your displayed fate. Android’s share interface then lets you choose a third-party app or service. The accompanying text may include a Google Play link to Daily Fate.

The App does not transmit the image until you select a sharing destination. Once you share it, the selected third party processes the image, accompanying text, and any related metadata under its own privacy policy. The temporary image may remain in the App’s cache until it is overwritten, cleared by Android, cleared by you, or removed when the App is uninstalled.

8. Permissions Used by the App

The App may use:

  • Internet access: required for advertisements, analytics, Google Play Billing, and opening online links.
  • Post notifications: used only if you enable daily reminders.
  • Receive boot completed: used to restore enabled local reminders after the device restarts.
  • Temporary content-URI access: used to allow the sharing app you select to read the generated fate image. Access is granted only for the sharing operation.

The App does not request access to precise location, contacts, call logs, microphone, camera, or your general photo library.

Information described in this Policy may be used to:

  • provide and maintain the App’s features;

We do not use the text of your locally selected fate to build a personal profile about you.
