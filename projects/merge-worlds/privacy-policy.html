Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In development

Merge Worlds has not yet been publicly released. This initial policy describes the game's planned use of Firebase and Google AdMob. We will update it as the game's features and data practices are finalized.

This Privacy Policy explains how Merge Worlds (the “Game”), provided by RUSHERS ADR (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use it, including any testing versions.

For privacy questions or requests, contact rushersadr@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features enabled, your device settings, and your privacy choices, the Game and its service providers may process:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and app-instance or device identifiers.
  • Usage and technical information: game interactions, sessions, performance information, and diagnostic logs, where the corresponding services are enabled.
  • Advertising information: IP address, approximate location inferred from it, advertising identifiers where available, ad impressions, and ad interactions.
  • Support communications: your email address and any information you choose to send when contacting us.

Game progress and preferences may also be stored on your device to support gameplay. The exact information processed will depend on the features included in the version you use.

2. Firebase

The Game uses Google Firebase to support its operation and improvement. Depending on the Firebase services enabled, Google may process app identifiers, device information, usage events, or technical diagnostics to help operate features, understand usage, and resolve issues.

The information collected depends on the particular Firebase services and their configuration. More information is available in Firebase's privacy and security documentation.

3. Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Ad personalization and data processing depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the advertising configuration. Where consent is required by applicable law, processing that relies on consent is subject to that choice.

You can manage available advertising privacy controls through your Android or Google account settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate and improve the Game, understand usage, maintain stability and security, provide and measure advertising, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations.

Google and its service partners may process information for the Firebase and AdMob services described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent abuse, or defend legal rights.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on providing the service you request, consent for optional processing where required, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Game, or legal obligations, as applicable.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Information is retained only as long as needed for its purpose, subject to legal requirements and the retention settings and policies of the relevant service provider. Google may process information in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its privacy documentation.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the Game can remove locally stored information. It does not necessarily remove information held by Google or included in device backups.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a data-protection authority.

To make a request concerning information under our control, email rushersadr@gmail.com. We may need additional information to verify and handle your request. For information held directly by Google, you may also need to use Google's privacy controls or contact Google.

Available app, device, or Google controls may allow you to change advertising preferences or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

7. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Merge Worlds develops, its services change, or legal requirements evolve. Updates will appear on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required by law.
