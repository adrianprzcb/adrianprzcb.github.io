Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 22 October 2026

Merge Worlds (“the Game”) is provided by RUSHERS Adr (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game stores and processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to the Android application with package name com.adrian.mergeworlds.

For privacy questions or requests, contact rushersadr@gmail.com.

1. Overview

You can play Merge Worlds without creating an account with us. Your progress and preferences are stored on your device. Connecting to Google Play Games for cloud backups is optional.

The Game uses:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase to understand gameplay and improve the Game.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Google AdMob to display advertisements.
  • Google Play Games Services, when you choose to connect, to save and restore progress.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information needed to operate and preserve your progress, including:

  • Unlocked worlds, levels, experience points and discovered recipes.
  • Coins, energy, stored refills and regeneration timestamps.
  • Items on your boards, customer orders and gameplay counters.
  • Gameplay statistics and time played.
  • Language, sound and music preferences.

This information remains on your device until it is replaced or removed. Clearing the Game’s app storage or uninstalling it normally removes its local data.

The Game disables Android’s automatic app-data backup. Optional Google Play Games backups are separate and are not deleted by uninstalling the Game.

3. Usage and gameplay statistics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how features are used, evaluate progression and balancing, and improve the playing experience.

Recorded events include starting a game mode, completing orders, discovering recipes, gaining levels, unlocking worlds and using energy refills.

Google Analytics may also process app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, language, sessions, app interactions, and approximate location derived from an IP address.

Analytics collection is enabled automatically in the current version and does not have an in-game opt-out switch. Advertising privacy choices are handled separately.

The Game does not attach your name, email address, Google password, Google Play Games player ID, or complete saved game to its custom Analytics events. Its Firebase Analytics configuration disables advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization signals.

4. Crash reports and diagnostics

The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, application-not-responding errors and related technical problems.

Reports may include stack traces, application state, app version, operating-system and device information, installation identifiers, and records of actions preceding a crash.

Crash reporting is enabled automatically. The Game does not attach a custom account identifier or complete saved game to Crashlytics reports.

This information helps us investigate errors, understand their impact and improve stability.

5. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements that can refill energy, and interstitial advertisements when entering Zen mode.

Rewarded advertisements are shown when you choose the corresponding option. Advertising-related data processing can also occur when the advertising service initializes or requests an advertisement, even if you do not watch a rewarded advertisement.

Depending on your region, device, privacy choices and Google’s implementation, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers, app-set identifiers and other device or account-related identifiers where applicable.
  • Device, operating-system, language and app information.
  • Advertisement requests, impressions, taps and video interactions.
  • Diagnostic information and information used to prevent advertising fraud.

This information is used to deliver and measure advertisements, provide advertising analytics, and detect fraud or abuse.

Where applicable, the Game displays Google’s advertising privacy messages. You can revisit advertising privacy choices in Settings when that option is available. You can also manage your advertising identifier and related controls through Android or Google settings.

Changing advertising preferences does not disable the Game’s separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics collection.

6. Optional Google Play Games cloud backups

You may choose to connect Google Play Games from the Game’s Settings. A connection is not required for local play.

When connected, Google processes the player identity and authentication information needed to provide this service. The Game does not receive your Google password.

Cloud backups can include coins, energy, unlocked worlds, levels, experience, discoveries, boards, orders, gameplay statistics and related progress.

Backups and restoration are initiated through the controls in Settings. Connecting an account alone does not automatically upload your progress. Stored refill credits are excluded from cloud backups.

The Game uses Google Play Games for saved progress; it does not submit achievements or leaderboard scores.

Google manages the account and cloud infrastructure. You can manage or delete the Game’s Play Games data through your Google Play Games settings. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete cloud backups. Google’s deletion instructions

7. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use this information to respond, investigate the reported issue and handle your request.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

8. How information is used and disclosed

Information is processed to:

  • Operate the Game and preserve progress.
  • Provide optional cloud backups and restoration.
  • Understand usage and improve gameplay.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Deliver, measure and protect advertising.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect against misuse.

Google processes information through the services described above. Advertising information may also be processed by Google’s advertising partners under the applicable privacy choices and policies.

We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or defend legal rights.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain advertising disclosures may be considered a “sale”, “sharing” or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. Available rights and controls depend on your location.

9. Data retention

Local progress remains on your device until removed or replaced. Optional cloud backups remain subject to your Google account controls and Google’s retention practices.

Analytics information is retained according to the applicable Google Analytics retention settings and policies.

Firebase states that Crashlytics retains crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning their removal from live and backup systems. Firebase retention information

Advertising information is retained according to Google’s applicable policies and legal requirements.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve issues, maintain appropriate records and meet legal obligations.

Removing the Game does not automatically delete information already held by Google or previously submitted support communications.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can:

  • Play without connecting Google Play Games.
  • Choose whether to watch rewarded advertisements.
  • Manage available advertising privacy choices.
  • Manage or delete your Android advertising identifier.
  • Delete Play Games data through Google’s controls.
  • Remove local progress by clearing the Game’s app storage.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to a data-protection authority.

To make a request, email rushersadr@gmail.com. Explain the request and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data. We may request proportionate verification.

We will handle requests as required by applicable law. Because much of the Game’s data uses installation identifiers rather than a named developer account, we may need additional information to locate it. Some information is controlled directly by Google and may also require using Google’s privacy tools.

11. Security and international processing

The Google services used by the Game encrypt transmitted information using HTTPS/TLS. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google and its service providers may process information outside your country. Their applicable terms describe the safeguards used for international transfers.

12. Children’s privacy

Parents or guardians who believe that a child’s personal information has been processed inappropriately through the Game should contact rushersadr@gmail.com.

We will investigate and take appropriate action under applicable law. Google services may also apply age restrictions, parental controls and consent requirements.
