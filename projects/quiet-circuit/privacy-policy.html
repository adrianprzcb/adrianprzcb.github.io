Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 22, 2026

Quiet Circuit: Idle Network (the “Game”) is provided by RUSHERS Adr (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored or processed when you use the Game, why it is used, when it may be shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

If you have questions or want to exercise a privacy right, contact us at adrianperezcb@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You can play without creating a separate account with us.
  • Your save data and settings are stored on your device and may be included in Android backup or device-transfer services.
  • Google Play Games sign-in and cloud saving are optional.
  • The Game uses Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay and app performance.
  • The Game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. Ads are not required for core progression.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information needed to operate and preserve your progress, including:

  • game progress, resources, node levels, Protocols, achievements, discoveries, statistics, and play-time information;
  • audio, haptic, language, notification, and other preferences;
  • timestamps used to calculate offline progress and timed effects;
  • purchase status and purchase tokens needed to grant and prevent duplicate delivery of purchased items; and
  • whether tutorials, review prompts, or similar in-game notices have already been shown.

This information is stored locally using Android storage technologies. Android may include the Game’s local database and settings in a device backup or device-to-device transfer when those services are enabled. Android and Google control the operation and retention of those backups.

Clearing the Game’s storage or uninstalling it normally removes local data from the device, but data contained in an Android backup may later be restored. Data stored through Google Play Games or other Google services is not necessarily deleted when the Game is uninstalled.

3. Firebase Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand how the Game is used and improve balancing, stability, and features.

Analytics events may include actions such as:

  • unlocking or upgrading a node;
  • resolving an anomaly;
  • claiming offline progress;
  • completing a Reboot or upgrading a Protocol;
  • purchasing or activating a Mega Charge; and
  • unlocking an achievement.

Depending on your device, settings, region, and Google’s implementation, Firebase may also process information such as an app-instance identifier, device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information. The Game does not send your name, email address, cloud-save contents, payment-card details, or Google password to Firebase Analytics as custom analytics events.

We use this information to measure feature usage, identify technical or balancing problems, and improve the Game. Google processes this information on our behalf under its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

4. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements. Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. If you choose to watch one, you may receive the reward described in the Game.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may initialize when the Game starts, even if you do not choose to watch a rewarded advertisement. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and advertising settings, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • advertising or other device identifiers, where available;
  • device, operating-system, language, and app information;
  • advertisement requests, impressions, interactions, and rewards; and
  • diagnostic, fraud-prevention, and performance information.

Google may use this information to provide, limit, personalize, measure, and protect advertising, subject to applicable law and your consent or device settings. Where consent is legally required, advertising-related processing that depends on consent should occur only after the required choice has been obtained.

You can manage advertising privacy controls through your Android or Google account settings. Resetting or deleting your advertising identifier may limit how it is used, but it does not remove information already retained by a service provider where retention is otherwise permitted.

5. Optional Google Play Games features

You may optionally connect the Game to Google Play Games. If you do, Google may process your Google Play Games player identity and related account information. The Game may then use Google Play Games to:

  • save and restore gameplay progress in the cloud;
  • unlock and display achievements;
  • submit your Architecture Points score to the leaderboard; and
  • record Game Stats, including gameplay progress, lifetime Signal, highest Signal per second, active play time, claimed offline time, Reboots, Architecture Points earned, and anomalies resolved.

Cloud saves can contain gameplay progress, node and Protocol levels, achievement and discovery history, gameplay statistics, and timestamps. Purchased Mega Charges, active paid-boost time, and processed purchase tokens are intentionally kept on the device and are not included in the Google Play Games cloud-save payload.

Google controls the Google Play Games account, player profile, leaderboard, achievements, Game Stats, and cloud infrastructure. You can manage or delete Play Games data through your Google account and Google Play Games privacy settings. Disabling cloud sync in the Game stops new sync requests from the Game but does not automatically delete information already stored by Google.

6. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment information and the transaction. We do not receive your full credit- or debit-card number.

The Game receives limited purchase information required to provide the purchased item, such as the product identifier, purchase status, quantity, and a purchase token. Purchase tokens may be stored locally to deliver a purchase once and prevent duplicate delivery. Google retains transaction records according to its own terms, legal obligations, and privacy policy.

7. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the Game can schedule local notifications for offline-progress limits and optional daily reminders. Notification preferences and scheduling information are stored on your device. The Game does not use these notifications to collect message contents, contacts, or precise location.

You can disable notifications at any time in the Game’s settings or in Android system settings.

8. Information you provide directly

The Game does not require you to provide a name, email address, or separate account. If you contact us for support or a privacy request, we will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to your request, provide support, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
